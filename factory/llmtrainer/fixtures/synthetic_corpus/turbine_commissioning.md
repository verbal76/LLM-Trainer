# Synthetic Fixture: Marnex Turbine Commissioning Checklist

This document is a synthetic test fixture invented for pipeline validation; the Marnex turbine does not exist and nothing here is real technical guidance.

## Pre-Start Checks

The commissioning engineer must verify that all guard covers are fitted and every fastener carries a witness mark. Oil temperature should be above the minimum start value displayed on the control panel. Confirm that the emergency stop circuit has been proven by a witnessed test before any rotation is attempted.

## First Rotation

Initial rotation must be performed at the lowest permitted speed while an observer monitors the vibration display. Any vibration reading above the amber threshold requires an immediate stop and a review of the alignment records. Record the coast-down time in the commissioning log for later comparison.

## Handover

At handover the engineer should supply the operator with the signed checklist and the final alignment report. Open punch-list items must be listed with an owner and a target date.

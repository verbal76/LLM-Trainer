# Synthetic Fixture: Orvell Coupling Alignment Primer

This document is a synthetic test fixture invented for pipeline validation; the Orvell coupling does not exist and nothing here is real technical guidance.

## Dial Indicator Method

Mount the dial indicator on the driver hub and sweep the driven hub through a full rotation while recording the readings. Angular and parallel misalignment should be calculated separately so that each can be corrected in turn. Repeat the sweep after every shim change to confirm the correction moved in the expected direction.

## Soft Foot

A soft foot condition exists when one mounting foot lifts more than the allowed gap after its bolt is loosened. Correct soft foot before alignment because a rocking machine will invalidate every later reading.

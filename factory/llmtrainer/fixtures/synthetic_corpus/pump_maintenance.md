# Synthetic Fixture: Gleebor Pump Maintenance Notes

This document is a synthetic test fixture invented for pipeline validation; the Gleebor pump does not exist and nothing here is real technical guidance.

## Daily Inspection

Before each shift the operator should check the Gleebor pump housing for visible cracks or fluid residue. A hairline crack in the housing means the pump must be tagged out and reported to the maintenance lead. The sight glass should show the lubricant level between the two etched marks on the casing.

## Seal Replacement

Replacing the shaft seal requires draining the lubricant reservoir into the approved catch pan first. The old seal should be pressed out slowly so the bearing seat is not scored by the tool. After installation the technician must rotate the shaft by hand for ten full turns to confirm smooth motion.

## Storage

Spare seals should be stored flat in a cool dry cabinet away from direct sunlight and solvents. Each package must carry a label showing the date of receipt so older stock is used first.

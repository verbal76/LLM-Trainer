# Synthetic Fixture: Quillon Valve Troubleshooting Guide

This document is a synthetic test fixture invented for pipeline validation; the Quillon valve does not exist and nothing here is real technical guidance.

## Sticking Actuator

If the actuator arm moves slowly or stops partway, first confirm that the supply pressure gauge reads inside the green band. A sticking arm with normal pressure usually indicates dried lubricant on the guide rod. Wipe the guide rod clean and apply a thin film of the approved lubricant before retesting.

## Leak at the Bonnet

A slow weep at the bonnet joint often points to an uneven tightening pattern rather than a failed gasket. The technician should loosen all bonnet bolts and retighten them in a cross pattern in three equal stages. A hairline crack in the housing means the pump must be tagged out and reported to the maintenance lead.

## Noisy Operation

A rattling sound during cycling is commonly caused by a loose stem nut inside the yoke assembly. Shut off the supply, vent the line, and inspect the nut for thread wear before reassembly.
